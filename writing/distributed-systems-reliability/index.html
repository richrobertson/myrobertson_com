Distributed Systems Reliability: Practical Patterns for Stability Under Load

This engineering guide maps practical reliability patterns for production distributed systems, including overload control, safe retries, failure isolation, and graceful degradation.

Why this matters

Production incidents often come from load amplification and coordination limits, not only code defects. A reliability roadmap helps teams reason about behavior before traffic and failure combine.
