Oracle Customer Notification Service Migration to OCI

Distributed systems migration case study focused on production reliability, controlled rollout, and operational stability.

Impact Signals

32 global data centers $2M enterprise deal support OCI-native routing and workers

Architecture Overview

Legacy notification paths transitioned to OCI ingress, routing, queueing, worker isolation, and observability-backed rollout checkpoints.

View diagram on homepage flagship artifact.

What This Migration Required

Core problem

Legacy pathways constrained supportability and rollout control during a customer-critical timeline.

My role

Led migration execution and backend implementation planning across the service transition.

Key engineering decisions

  • Controlled traffic shifts instead of all-at-once cutover
  • Queueing and worker isolation to reduce overload amplification
  • Observability-first rollout with explicit metrics checkpoints

Tradeoffs

Balanced migration speed against production safety and support continuity.

Outcome

Improved supportability and delivery stability while supporting a $2M enterprise deal.
