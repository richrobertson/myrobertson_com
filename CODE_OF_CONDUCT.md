# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This repository is a professional space for constructive collaboration.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback specific, technical, and actionable.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus on improving the work, not criticizing people.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct and may restrict participation when needed.

## Reporting

Report behavior concerns through repository maintainers via private channels listed on the site contact page.
